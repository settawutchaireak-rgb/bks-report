# Dashboard background assets v51

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Four original PNGs are retained in design-sources/ and four optimized WebP files are used by the website in assets/weeklyStat0-v51.webp through assets/weeklyStat3-v51.webp.

## Exact prompt set

### Card 1

Create a single production website dashboard card BACKGROUND asset only. Panoramic landscape 3:1 aspect ratio, 1536x512 if possible. Sophisticated photorealistic architectural business photography, soft ice-blue and white palette, pale distant city skyline, reflective pale floor, daylight, restrained natural realism. Style inspired by clean bus terminal and shopping arcade dashboard backgrounds: bright elegant airy, no sci-fi, no neon, no decorative wave ribbons, no coins, no overdramatic sunset. Full bleed photo with absolutely no border, no rounded corner, no white margin. Left 55% is low-contrast photographic detail faded with soft natural light (not empty solid white) allowing dark navy title and numbers to be overlaid later in HTML. Main subject sits on far right 40%, entire subject fits. NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO LOGOS, NO ICONS, NO UI, NO CHART LABELS. Subject for card 1, total fee revenue: a realistic clean white and navy intercity coach bus parked beside a modern steel and glass bus terminal on the far right, understated city skyline and reflected terminal structures on the left. Photo should feel credible for a Thai transport management report.
