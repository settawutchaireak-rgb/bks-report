The derived geographic SVG files `assets/thailand-region-0-v45.svg` through `assets/thailand-region-4-v45.svg` are distributed under ODbL 1.0. Their path coordinates are embedded in the SVG files and available in this public repository. This geographic-data license applies to the map derivatives, not unrelated website code or user-supplied photographs.

The maps retain real provincial geometry and a consistent geographic projection. They are display maps, not survey or navigation products. Each SVG contains 77 province paths, with a white country and the selected display region highlighted in pink.

Display groups use a five-region convention:

- Central, including western provinces: remaining 19 provinces.
- East: Chachoengsao, Chanthaburi, Chon Buri, Prachin Buri, Rayong, Sa Kaeo, Trat (7).
- Northeast: 20 provinces, ISO codes TH-30 through TH-49.
- North, including the lower north: Chiang Mai, Chiang Rai, Kamphaeng Phet, Lampang, Lamphun, Mae Hong Son, Nakhon Sawan, Nan, Phayao, Phetchabun, Phichit, Phitsanulok, Phrae, Sukhothai, Tak, Uthai Thani, Uttaradit (17).
- South: Chumphon, Krabi, Nakhon Si Thammarat, Narathiwat, Pattani, Phangnga, Phatthalung, Phuket, Ranong, Satun, Songkhla, Surat Thani, Trang, Yala (14).

This display grouping can be adjusted if the reporting department uses a different provincial grouping. Trip figures remain labeled as examples until the source cells are confirmed.
